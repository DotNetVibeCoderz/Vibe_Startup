aplikasi manajemen logistik seperti Tiki, JNE, atau Paxel. Dengan fitur:

---

 🚚 Core Features
- Order Management: Input pesanan, tracking status, integrasi barcode/QR.
- Shipment Tracking: Real-time GPS, status update otomatis, notifikasi pelanggan.
- Delivery Scheduling: Penjadwalan kurir, optimasi rute, estimasi waktu tiba.
- Pickup Request: Pelanggan bisa request pickup dari rumah/kantor.

---

 💳 Payment & Finance
- Multi-payment Integration: E-wallet, transfer bank, COD, kartu kredit.
- Invoice & Billing: Auto-generate invoice, e-receipt, laporan keuangan.
- Insurance Options: Tambahan proteksi barang, klaim otomatis.

---

 📦 Warehouse & Inventory
- Warehouse Management: Stok barang, lokasi penyimpanan, integrasi RFID.
- Inventory Tracking: Monitoring keluar/masuk barang, batch management.
- Packaging Optimization: Rekomendasi ukuran box, kalkulasi berat/volume.

---

 👥 Customer & Courier
- Customer Portal: Tracking pesanan, riwayat transaksi, loyalty program.
- Courier App: Navigasi rute, update status, komunikasi dengan pelanggan.
- Customer Support: Chatbot, live chat, tiket komplain.

---

 📊 Analytics & Dashboard
- Business Analytics: Volume pengiriman, SLA compliance, revenue tracking.
- Operational Dashboard: Monitoring kurir, warehouse, pickup/delivery.
- Trend Analysis: Prediksi permintaan, peak season, optimasi biaya.

---

 🔗 Integrations
- API Integration: Marketplace (Tokopedia, Shopee), ERP, CRM.
- Third-party Logistics: Partner shipping, cross-border delivery.
- IoT & Sensors: Smart locker, suhu barang sensitif, RFID tracking. Buatkan juga simulatornya jalan di thread berbeda.

---

 🔒 Security & Compliance
- Data Security: Enkripsi end-to-end, role-based access.
- Regulatory Compliance: Pajak, bea cukai, dokumen ekspor/impor.
- Audit Trail: Log aktivitas, histori perubahan status.

---

 🌐 Advanced Features
- AI Route Optimization: Machine learning untuk rute tercepat & hemat biaya.
- Dynamic Pricing: Tarif fleksibel berdasarkan jarak, waktu, demand.
- Green Logistics: Tracking emisi karbon, opsi eco-delivery.

---
